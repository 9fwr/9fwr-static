What is Data Thinking?

Data Thinking is a strategic approach designed to help companies develop comprehensive data strategies. It is an adaption of the well-known Design Thinking approach to Data. It focuses on understanding and leveraging data to optimize business processes such as sales, marketing, and fulfillment, ultimately aiming to increase profit, revenue, and customer base.

What is the Data Thinking Programme?


The Data Thinking program is a series of interviews with stakeholders together with a thourough analysis by our experts and a final common workshop to ideate data needs, KPIs and potential for data driven action within the  business' value generating processes.

It will answer central questions indispensable for a successful data setup:
‍
‍

Value generating business processes

What are the value creating processes in the company along the customer journey?

KPI & Metrics Tree

What KPI exists in these processes? How do these KPI depend on each other and what is the North Star metric?

Action

What actions can Stakeholders derive from data and how will that influence business success. What is the current state here and what would be the ideal situation/wishes?

Data collected

What data is collected along those processes? What is missing and still has to be implemented?

Data Infrastructure

What technical systems are needed and what are the first steps?

Risks

What legal, technical or organization risks exist for a successful data setup?

How it works

We work together with a Data Champion who will lead the project from your side and will direct other C-Level Stakeholders involved in the Data Project. The Programm will consist of a series of interviews with the participating department heads, collection of information from your team, preparation and facilitation the Workshop and delivery of the final data strategy.

Kick-off

We are working with a Data Champion from your company who will engage other stakeholders (e.g., COO, CSO, CTO, CFO, CEO, etc.) and ensure that all relevant business areas are covered. This needs to be someone at the C-level as well.

Interviews

A short interview (30 min to 1 hour) with the relevant stakeholders to capture current needs and the current state of data in the different deparments.

Collect Information

As result of the interviews we collect more details about processes and current data systems from team members

Data Thinking Canvas

We use the Data Thinking Canvas to bring collected information into context and prepare the workshop

Data Thinking Workshop

We bring all stakeholders to the table to ideate on the business value generating processes, their KPI, data driven actions to be taken, current state of data collection and infrastructure and potential risks. Usually a 2-4h online workshop though this can be adapted to your needs

Final Data Strategy

Based on the collaboratively developed Data Thinking Canvas, we deliver a comprehensive data strategy. This strategy highlights the potential of data-driven actions, decisions, and optimizations derived from KPIs. It also outlines the steps needed to implement a data infrastructure that supports these decision-making processes.
