Consulting

Opinionated Data Consulting

We listen and we challenge.  Working with our customers, we have seen things that work and that do not work, both organizationally and technically. As consultants, we see it as one of our most important tasks to challenge our customers' data systems and their data strategies. Nevertheless, we do listen and learn. We strive to partner with our customers on an equal footing and understand their business as part of their team. We use our knowledge to tailor the strategy and technical solutions to the exact needs and challenges of our common project.

Data Strategy

"How can we become data driven?" and "How can we leverage the potential of our data?" are common questions our customers ask. With our efficient Data Thinking process we define a lean and outcome-oriented route to use data to drive growth and value.

Insights

We translate Data to Insights and to Decisions. We understand business as well as technology and make sure the data is modeled and interpreted in the right way to support business decisions. We enable self-analytics through Business Intelligence tools or deep dive into data to search for hidden insights and correlations.

Data Ops

We have thorough experience in building modern, performant, scalable, efficient and lean cloud infrastructure for big- and mid-sized data products serving enterprise to startup customers.

TECH

Our Tech Stack

We are advocates of the the modern data stack. Cloud-based data warehouse, SaaS before custom ETL, version controlled data modeling, unified metrics layer, lean BI setup. We are versatile in the tools we and our customers are using. Here a collection of tools that we find beneficial for most data cases.

The 9 friendly white rabbits are a data & analytics agency and consultancy supporting customers from Enterprises to Startups building their data stacks.

We work with technologies in web analytics / web tracking including Google Analytics, Matomo, Google Tag Manager, server-side tracking, Javascript, HTTP, HTML and Rest API development.

In Data Engineering we support in SaaS ETL Tools like Fivetran, Supermetrics or Custom ETL using Airflow, Cloudfunctions, AWS Lambda functions, Cloud Run. We set up Big Data Infrastructure on Google Cloud, Amazon Web Services (AWS), Docker, Kubernetes and support in Data Warehouse Setups with Google BigQuery, Snowflake, Hadoop, SparkSQL, Databricks.

In Analytics, data modeling and Data Governance we use SQL, dbt, dataform and git, visualizing data with business intelligence tools like Tableau, PowerBI, Looker and Looker Studio as well as offering deep dives and data science projects in R, python, Jupyter and pandas.
